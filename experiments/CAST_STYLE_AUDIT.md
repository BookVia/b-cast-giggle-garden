| Character | Updated features | Preserved identity |
|---|---|---|
| Tebo | Anatomy-based face, short curl groom, connected hoodie/body, detailed shoes | Brown skin; blue dinosaur hoodie; khaki shorts; blue shoes |

Twelve views were rendered locally and validated as PNGs. Each blend was reopened; embedded reference, required identity components, finite mesh/hair coordinates and absence of rigs were checked. Detailed topology findings are in CAST_STYLE_AUDIT.json. These checks do not establish approved likeness or animation readiness.

Remaining work includes facial likeness, mouth/chin and neck contour, hairline/curl control, clothing folds, hands, modifier-stack optimization and deformation-oriented retopology.

Earlier V36–V38 files are preserved as superseded experiments. Scripts require the Windows project input blends and png_validation.py; they are not a standalone production pipeline.
